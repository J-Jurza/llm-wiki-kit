# P5: Ask the wiki

**When:** whenever you have a question that touches more than one page. This is what the wiki is for.

**What it produces:** an answer with wikilinks to the pages it came from, and an offer to keep the answer as a page.

## Paste this, with your question

```
Use the wiki-query skill. Question: [your question here]. Cite the pages you used. Tell me what the wiki does not know that would make the answer better. If the answer is worth keeping, offer to file it and say where.
```

Example questions that work well once the wiki has twenty or so pages:

- What do my sources disagree about on [topic]?

- What have I read that bears on [decision I am making]?

- Which of my goals has the least material behind it?

- Summarise what I know about [concept] for someone who has read none of it.

## The habit

File the good answers. An answer that joined three pages is a fourth page waiting to exist. Over months this is what turns a folder of summaries into a wiki.
