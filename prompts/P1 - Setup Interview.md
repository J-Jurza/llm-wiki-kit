# P1: Setup interview

**When:** once, after P0. This is the step that turns a generic template into your vault.

**Takes:** fifteen minutes of conversation. Answer in plain sentences, the agent does the writing.

**What it produces:** `Profile/About Me.md` filled in, your first Knowledge domains and Living areas named in the index, and a short list of what to put in the inbox first.

## Paste this

```
Run my setup interview for this knowledge vault. Rules: ask the questions in batches of three or four, wait for my answers, do not write anything until the interview is done, then show me the drafts before saving.

Interview me on:
- Who I am: what I do, where I live, how my weeks go.
- The areas of life I want this wiki to serve. Push me for specifics: "health" is too broad, "returning to running after a knee injury" is right.
- What I already read, watch and listen to, and in which formats (books, newsletters, podcasts, YouTube, courses). Which of these do I want captured?
- Three to five goals for the next twelve months, concrete enough that a page could track them.
- What stays private: topics you should treat with care or never ingest, and whether I will use the Private/ folder.
- How I want to be spoken to in the wiki: second person ("you"), or by name.

Then:
1. Draft Profile/About Me.md from my answers, in my words where you can. This is the one time you write to Profile/.
2. Propose three to five Knowledge domains and two to four Living areas, each with a one-line description, and ask me to confirm or rename. Do not create folders yet: the growth rule says a folder arrives at three pages. Add the confirmed names as headings in Wiki/_Meta/_index.md under 2. Knowledge and 3. Living.
3. Suggest five things I could put in Inbox/ this week that would seed those domains, from what I told you I already read and watch.
4. Append a structure entry to Wiki/_Meta/_log.md and, if git is on, commit "Setup interview: profile and first domains".

Finish with the five inbox suggestions and nothing else.
```

## What good looks like

An About Me page you would not be embarrassed to read back. Domain names you actually use for these things. If a proposed domain feels off, rename it now: renaming later means moving pages.
