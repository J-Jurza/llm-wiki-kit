# P3: First synthesis

**When:** the ingest report says a concept is covered by three or more source pages, or you have ingested about ten sources in one area.

**What it produces:** your first Knowledge page, organised by idea rather than by source, with every claim pointing at the source it came from.

## Paste this

```
Read Wiki/_Meta/_index.md and tell me which concepts are now covered by three or more source pages. Pick the one you think is most ready and say why. Then run the wiki-synthesise skill on it.

When the page is written, show me: the sources it drew on, one place where the sources agree, one place where they disagree and how you handled it, and any claim you had to mark as coming from outside the vault's sources.
```

## What good looks like

A page you could hand to a friend as "here is what I have learned about X", with wikilinks they could follow to the evidence. If the page reads like three book summaries stapled together, say so: "organise this by idea, not by source" is the fix.
