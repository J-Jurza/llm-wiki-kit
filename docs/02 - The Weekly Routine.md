# The weekly routine

Twenty minutes a week keeps the wiki alive. Less than that and the inbox becomes a graveyard, more than that and you are doing the agent's job.

## During the week: collect

- **Clip** anything you finished reading and want to keep. One click with the Web Clipper, it lands in `Inbox/Clippings/`.

- **Drop** PDFs, exported notes, transcripts into `Inbox/`. Name them so the agent can tell what they are.

- **Write** a paragraph of your own thinking when something clicks, as a note in `Inbox/`. Your own synthesis is the most valuable source there is.

- **Ignore** the urge to file things into `Wiki/` yourself. The inbox is the only door.

## Weekly: compile

Paste `prompts/P6 - Weekly Routine.md`. It processes the inbox, asks whether anything is ready to synthesise, checks for stale Living pages and suggests one question. Answer its two questions. Skim what it wrote.

## Monthly: check

Add the lint line from P6. Pick two or three fixes from the report. Do not try to clear every warning: the wiki is allowed to be uneven, it is not allowed to be broken.

## When you have a question

`prompts/P5 - Ask the Wiki.md`. File the good answers.

## What the agent will not do unless you ask

Delete anything outside the inbox. Move a page out of `Wiki/`. Edit your profile. Create a new top-level folder. Read `Private/` (even if you ask).

## Signs it is working

After a month: twenty or so source pages, two or three Knowledge pages, an index you can read top to bottom. After three months: you ask it something and the answer joins pages you had forgotten you had. After six: the Living layer holds plans and decisions you actually follow.

## Signs it is not

The inbox has thirty items in it. A Knowledge page reads like three summaries stapled together. You are editing wiki pages by hand. Each of these has a one-line fix: run P6, say "organise by idea not by source", and stop.

## Obsidian extras worth turning on

- **Graph view** (the dots icon): the shape of the wiki. Hubs are the big nodes, orphans float alone. A quick visual lint.

- **Attachments folder** set to a fixed subfolder (the install step did this) plus the command **Download attachments for current file**, bound to a hotkey. Clipped pages keep their images locally, and the agent can look at them when asked.

- **Dataview** community plugin, later: tables over page frontmatter (every Living page with Updated older than three months, every source by type). Optional. The index does the same job by hand until the wiki is large.

- **Git**: every change the agent makes is a commit. "What did it change last Tuesday" has an answer.
